# DAYFLOW Employee Attendance Policy 2026

## 1. Core Working Hours
Standard working hours are **09:00 AM to 06:00 PM (8 working hours + 1 hour lunch break)**, Monday through Friday.

## 2. Check-in and Grace Period
- Standard check-in time is **09:00 AM**.
- A grace period of **15 minutes** (up to 09:15 AM) is permitted without penalty.
- Check-ins recorded between 09:16 AM and 10:30 AM are marked as **LATE**.
- 3 late check-ins in a single calendar month trigger an automatic warning notification.

## 3. Half-Day and Short Shifts
- Working between 4 and 6 hours in a single working day is classified as a **HALF_DAY**.
- Working fewer than 4 hours without prior leave approval is marked as **ABSENT**.

## 4. Unannounced Absences
Failure to check in or submit a leave request by 11:30 AM on a working day will trigger an **UNANNOUNCED_ABSENCE** alert sent to the department manager.
