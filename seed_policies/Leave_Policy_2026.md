# DAYFLOW Employee Leave Policy 2026

## 1. General Overview
This policy governs employee leave entitlements, application procedures, and approval criteria for all full-time employees at DAYFLOW Technologies.

## 2. Leave Categories and Annual Allowances
- **Paid Annual Leave (PAID):** Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave (SICK):** Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Medical certificates are mandatory for sick leaves exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- **Unpaid Leave (UNPAID):** Subject to approval by the Department Head and HR when paid leave balances are exhausted.

## 3. Notice Period Requirements
- **Short Leave (1-2 days):** Must be submitted at least **24 hours** in advance.
- **Extended Leave (3-5 days):** Must be submitted at least **5 working days** in advance.
- **Long Leave (6+ days):** Must be submitted at least **14 calendar days** in advance.

## 4. Blackout Periods and Constraints
- Leave applications during year-end freeze periods (December 20 to January 5) require HR Director authorization.
- No more than 30% of employees in a single engineering or operations pod may be on leave simultaneously.

## 5. Roll-over and Expiry
- A maximum of 5 unused Paid Leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried forward leave must be used by March 31.
